What Wine will be

Built around intention, not volume

Wine is still being built, and it is not live yet. Everything below is planned for launch — described honestly, so you know exactly what you would be joining the waitlist for.

  • Planned

    Intention-first matching

    You say what you are actually looking for, and Wine shows you people who want the same thing — a real relationship, something meaningful, or clarity before labels. No endless swiping, no popularity scores.

  • Planned

    Prompt React

    Instead of a generic tap, you react to a specific prompt on someone's profile with a short, real note. If they accept, that note becomes your first message — so every match starts with something worth saying.

  • Planned

    Wingman

    A thoughtful second pair of eyes on your connections. Wingman reads only aggregate signals, never decides for you, and never gives anyone a score — it offers a plain-language observation and one suggested next step.

  • Planned

    Photo Verified, honestly defined

    A Photo Verified badge will mean one thing only: the person's selfie was matched to their profile photos. Nothing more is claimed, and nothing more should be read into it.

  • Planned

    Safety is never paywalled

    Blocking, reporting, date-planning with a trusted contact, and SOS help will be free for everyone, always. Feeling safe on a date is not a premium feature.

  • Planned

    No payments or gifts in the app

    Wine will not sell gifts, boosts, or paid attention. Nobody can pay to reach you, and nobody can pay to be seen by you.

The waitlist

What happens next?

  1. 1

    You join via the form

    Joining takes under a minute — your email and where you are in the world, nothing more.

  2. 2

    Wine emails you — only about Wine

    With your consent, you hear from Wine only about its launch and beta. No partner offers, no unrelated mail.

  3. 3

    Beta invites go out in batches

    Invites will roll out in small batches globally, so early members — including our early community in India — get a considered experience rather than a crowded launch.

  4. 4

    No spam, ever

    Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes one tap. If you leave the list, Wine stops writing.
